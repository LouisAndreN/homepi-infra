HomePi 1.0.0 is earned by a measurement, not given on a date (docs/versioning.md): a full restore
drill on a blank disk with no error at all.

## The measurement: game day C8

The production NVMe was removed. The platform was rebuilt on a spare disk from this signed
repository and the age-encrypted archives in S3 only, production came back, and the key of the
rebuilt Pi left SOPS. Every step ran once, with no code change.

| Measure | Value | Target |
|---|---|---|
| RTO (production off to every check green on the rebuilt Pi) | **48 min 08 s** | 4 h |
| Sensor data lost | 21 min 45 s | 1 h |
| Certificates re-issued | 0 (7 ESP32s reconnected as themselves) | 0 |
| Phases at their first attempt | 20 of 20, then R7, R9, R8 and the SOPS step | all |

Full report: [docs/game-days/2026-11-24-c8.md](https://github.com/LouisAndreN/homepi-infra/blob/v1.0.0/docs/game-days/2026-11-24-c8.md).

## What 1.0.0 covers

- Pull GitOps from signed tags only, checked on the Pi before the tag is signed, with automatic
  rollback for the services that support it and a hold after two failed applies (ADR 0001).
- An isolated IoT network, a private PKI with device identity bound to a manufacturer certificate,
  MQTT over mTLS with a CRL enforced at the broker, signed OTA with a staged rollout.
- Observability with tested alert rules and an independent dead man's switch through AWS.
- Hourly off-site backups to a four-account AWS organization, and a restore procedure that is
  proven, end to end, by the drill above.

## What it does not cover

Single node, no high availability; disk encryption that does not protect against theft (headless
boot); no out-of-band access. The full list, with what was observed: `docs/known-limits.md`.

What each release changed: `CHANGELOG.md`.
