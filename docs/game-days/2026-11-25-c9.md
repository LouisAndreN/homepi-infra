## Defects

1. **The boot catch-up of a missed backup runs before InfluxDB answers.** Production was off at 03:50;
   at boot the native backup timer (`Persistent=true`) caught up one second after systemd reported
   InfluxDB started, and the backup failed on `connection refused` to `127.0.0.1:8086/health`.
   systemd's "started" for InfluxDB is the process, not the API. Any restart of production after
   03:50 hits it, not only drills. C8 could not see it: production came back before 01:00. Fix: the
   backup waits for `/health` (bounded) before running.
2. **The drill tag date in `CHANGELOG.md` can be the day before the tag.** The fix written before C9
   checked the date at the start of R4TAG (23:56:15); the tag was signed at 00:03:03, after the
   7-minute precheck. `CHANGELOG.md` says 2026-11-25, the tag 2026-11-26, and the CI `changelog` job
   of v1.1.5 failed. Fix: date the entries from the tagged commit, not from the tag.
3. **CI `tests` failed on the v1.1.6 tag** (root stage, after 2 seconds), while the same commit
   passed on `main` minutes earlier and the stage passes locally on the tag. The log needs a GitHub
   login; the job is to be re-run and its log read before a cause is named.

## The error budget

| SLO | Before T0 | After R8 | Bad minutes counted | Budget (28 days) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ingestion | 1 | -1.53 | about 511 | 202 min |
| Device API | 1 | -0.27 | about 510 | 403 min |

The plan predicted about 30% of the ingestion budget **for an hour off**. Production was off 8 h
31 min, because nothing was done between R6 (00:13) and the morning. Per hour off, the drill spent
511 / 8.52 = 60 bad minutes, 29.7% of the budget: the per-hour prediction holds; the drill duration
did not. The written rule (budget above 0 after R8) fails, and its cause is the pause, not the SLO
design. The plan wrote "FAIL of the SLO design" for this case; that attribution was wrong, the
result is not changed.

Consequence, by the policy of `docs/slo.md`: no release that is not a fix for these SLOs until the
budgets are positive again, when the bad minutes of 2026-11-25 and 26 leave the 28-day window
(around 2026-12-23). v1.1.6 (the drill key out of SOPS) was released anyway, as a security
exception: the drill key opened the secrets of the tags released during the drill.

## What the numbers do not say

- The sensor data the spare collected during the 8 hours stays out of production. Its hourly deltas
  are in S3 (`homepi-r5-deltas` can replay them); not done.
- The production SLI counts the whole night as bad, although the spare served the devices. The
  budget measures production, not the service.
- The spare made the nightly backups of 2026-11-26 under the production name; production made its
  own the same morning (state backup catch-up 08:02, native by hand, off-site 08:10).
- R4TAG took 59 s more than in C8, close to the "about a minute" the plan predicted for the alert
  and inhibition tests in the precheck.
